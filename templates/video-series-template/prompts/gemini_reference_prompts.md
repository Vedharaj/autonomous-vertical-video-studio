# Gemini Vision Reference Image Prompts Guide

> Deterministic prompt formulas for generating 9:16 vertical shot reference frames (`epXX_shotYY_ref.png`) with strict single-perspective composition guards.

---

## Standard Prompt Template

```text
3D stylized Pixar-meets-anime chibi aesthetic, 9:16 vertical ratio.
[CAMERA PERSPECTIVE]: Single camera viewpoint (e.g. Low-angle medium shot / Over-the-shoulder looking forward).
[SUBJECT]: [Character name], oversized round head, short stubby chibi proportions (1:2 head-to-body ratio), dressed in [costume details, props].
[EXPRESSION & POSE]: [Exact behavioral pose, eye gaze, mouth shape, emotion].
[ENVIRONMENT]: Dusty village cricket ground in rural South India, coconut palm trees in distant hazy background, handmade wooden scoreboard, natural warm golden daylight.
[LIGHTING & PALETTE]: Warm golden hour sun, soft diffuse rim light, high vibrancy.
[NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS]: Single unified composition, absolutely NO split screen, NO multi-panel collage, NO duplicated characters, NO motion trail lines, NO text, NO speech bubbles, NO comic book frames.
```
